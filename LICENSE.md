# Source and release status

This is a Private candidate, not an automatic grant to publish all included
YamRail-derived source. Original YamRail components and new adapters retain
their ownership and source maturity. A separate owner/Human release artifact
is required before external disclosure or submission.

Third-party components retain their original licenses and notices. Consult
LICENSES_AND_PROVENANCE.csv, DEPENDENCIES.json, dependency_licenses/ and pinned
font/vendor/source notices. No Microsoft Office/AutoCAD binary, entitlement,
service token or license is included or transferred.

Recovered materials whose redistribution rights are UNKNOWN are metadata-only
in the judge candidate, with their exclusions and alternatives recorded. A
source hash is provenance, not a license grant.
